Made for night owls with mornings ahead.

Beddy Butler is a charming menu bar companion that helps you finish the evening and make your way to bed. Set a bedtime window, then carry on. When it is time to wind down, your Butler offers a timely nudge without taking over your screen.

Choose Shy for a polite suggestion, Insistent for firmer encouragement, or Zombie for a chilling forewarning of how you might feel tomorrow. Progressive mode moves naturally between personalities as bedtime approaches.

YOUR EVENING, BEAUTIFULLY

• Lives quietly in the macOS menu bar
• Shows the next nudge and tonight's schedule at a glance
• Lets you hear a sample, snooze, pause, or make a one-night adjustment in seconds
• Saves changes instantly

BUILT AROUND REAL LIFE

• Choose exactly which nights each schedule uses
• Add a second schedule for weekends, religious observance, shared routines, or any other pattern
• Follow a rotating cycle for shift work and changing rosters
• Travel freely with automatic time zone and daylight-saving adjustments

DESIGNED TO BE NOTICED, BUT NOT GET IN YOUR WAY

• Hear the original voiced reminders
• Use a persistent visual menu bar badge instead of sound
• Combine sound and visual cues
• Add optional silent Notification Centre alerts with Acknowledge, Snooze, and Pause actions
• Set the voice volume independently

COMPLETELY FREE AND PRIVATE

No account. No advertising. No analytics. No subscription. No tracking. Your schedule stays on your Mac.

Lovingly rebuilt for modern macOS in Swift 6, with system materials, dark mode, Reduced Motion, Reduced Transparency, Full Keyboard Access, and VoiceOver support.
