Meet Beddy Butler, a playful little presence in your Mac's menu bar who helps you stop working and make your way to bed.

Choose a bedtime window and let your Butler offer well timed encouragement throughout the evening. Start with a shy suggestion, ask for something more insistent, or enable Progressive mode and let the personality grow more determined as bedtime approaches.

BUILT AROUND YOUR LIFE

• Choose exactly which nights each schedule applies
• Add a second schedule for weekends, religious observance, shared routines, or any other pattern
• Use rotating cycles for shift work and changing rosters
• Make a one night adjustment without disturbing your regular routine
• Travel freely, with automatic time zone and daylight saving recalculation

NUDGES YOU CAN NOTICE

• Hear the original voiced Butler reminders
• Use a persistent visual menu bar badge instead of sound
• Combine sound and visual cues
• Add optional silent Notification Centre alerts with Acknowledge, Snooze, and Pause actions
• Adjust the voice volume independently

SMALL, PRIVATE, AND DELIGHTFUL

Beddy Butler lives quietly in the menu bar, uses no account, contains no advertising, and sends no analytics. Your schedule stays on your Mac.

The original 2016 idea has been lovingly rebuilt for modern macOS with a native Swift 6 interface, system materials, dark mode, Reduced Motion, Reduced Transparency, Full Keyboard Access, and VoiceOver support.
