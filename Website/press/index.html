Press kit

Beddy Butler at a glance.

An original, completely free macOS menu bar companion for ending the evening with a little more intention.

Short description

Beddy Butler lives in the Mac menu bar and offers timely bedtime nudges through original character voices, a persistent visual badge, or both. Flexible schedules support ordinary weeks, observance, rotating shifts, travel, and one night adjustments.

Product facts

  • Platform: macOS 13 Ventura or later.
  • Price: free, with no subscription or advertising.
  • Privacy: no account, analytics, tracking, or network entitlement.
  • Personalities: Shy, Insistent, and Zombie, with optional progressive escalation.
  • Accessibility: sound, visual, or combined nudges; silent local notifications; VoiceOver and keyboard support; Reduced Motion and Reduced Transparency adaptations.
  • Origin: first released in 2016, rebuilt in Swift 6 for modern macOS.
