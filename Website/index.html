Three manners of persuasion

How firmly may he insist?

Every voice is original. The restored library has been carefully balanced and the longest Zombie monologues have been split into mercifully short nudges.

A gentle hint

Shy

Soft, courteous encouragement for those who only need a nudge.

A firmer word

Insistent

Clearer direction when courtesy alone is not quite getting through.

The final resort

Zombie

The undead consequence of staying up far beyond any reasonable hour.

A silly little app, lovingly restored

Ten years later, the Butler returns.

Beddy Butler began in 2016 as a playful cure for nights that went on too long. The new edition keeps the original character and recordings, then rebuilds everything around them in native Swift 6 for modern macOS.

It is still silly. It is now also rather good.
